# Truth or Dare — Play Store description

🎉 Turn any night into a story with Truth or Dare — a colorful local party game made for friends, couples, and groups.

Add your players, choose who each person wants to interact with, pick a level, and let the animated spinner choose the next turn. Every player can choose Truth or Dare, hear a prompt aloud, choose another prompt, or finish the turn when everyone is ready.

🎭 FOUR WAYS TO PLAY

😎 Casual — easy, funny, and silly prompts
😍 Flirty — playful and bold prompts
😏 Hot — confident and intimate prompts [Premium]
😈 Naughty — the boldest and most daring prompts [Premium]

🎮 MADE FOR YOUR GROUP

• Add, edit, and remove players at any time
• Set each player's gender and interaction preferences
• Match with men, women, everyone, or selected specific players
• Use the fair animated spinner to choose each turn
• Avoid selecting the same player twice in a row
• Keep every eligible player involved before repeats in larger groups
• Collapse the player and level panels for a cleaner home screen

✨ EVERY TURN

• Choose Truth or Dare with dedicated blue and yellow buttons
• Get prompts adjusted to the selected level
• Choose another prompt whenever you want
• Use optional text-to-speech to read prompts aloud
• Leave a prompt screen and narration stops automatically

👑 PREMIUM FEATURES

Premium unlocks the complete game:

• 😏 Hot and 😈 Naughty levels
• ✍️ Create, edit, delete, import, export, and play custom truths and dares
• 🎚️ Set a 1–10 comfort-zone limit for each player
• 🔁 Limit consecutive Truth choices before a Dare is required
• 🔄 Enable Reverse Dare after a chosen number of consecutive dares
• ⚙️ Access every gameplay setting

Choose from available subscription or lifetime purchase options. Eligible purchases can be restored through Google Play. Verified Premium access can remain available temporarily while offline.

🎨 PERSONALIZE THE APP

• Dark and light themes
• Optional Android system color palette
• A bold dark-blue and orange identity
• Per-level colors and custom illustrated faces
• Support for Android themed launcher icons

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Truth or Dare works locally on your device. No account is required. The app does not use advertising or analytics and does not operate a remote gameplay server. Player details, preferences, custom cards, game progress, and settings stay in local app storage.

🤝 PLAY WITH RESPECT

Every prompt is optional. Anyone can skip, stop, or choose something else. Keep the game voluntary, consensual, respectful, and free from harm.
