# Truth or Dare — Google Play description

🎭 Make tonight a story with Truth or Dare, a private party game designed for friends, couples, and groups.

Add your players, choose who each person wants to interact with, select an intensity level, and let the animated spinner choose the next turn. Each player can choose Truth or Dare, request another prompt, and continue when everyone is ready.

🎉 FREE FEATURES

😎 Soft mode — easy, funny, and lighthearted prompts
😍 Hot mode — flirty and bold prompts
🎡 Fair randomized turns that avoid immediate repeats
👥 Player gender and interaction preferences
🎯 Match with men, women, everyone, or selected specific players
💙💛 Clearly color-coded Truth and Dare choices
🔊 Optional prompt narration using Android text-to-speech
🎨 Dark, light, and system themes with optional Android system colors
↕️ Collapsible player and level panels for a cleaner home screen
🔒 Local gameplay without accounts, advertising, or analytics

⭐ PREMIUM FEATURES

Premium unlocks every level and gameplay setting:

😉 Hard mode — confident and intimate prompts
😈 Extreme mode — the boldest and most daring prompts
✏️ My truths and dares — create, edit, delete, import, export, and play your own custom cards
🎚️ Comfort-zone calibration — set a 1–10 intensity limit for each player; targeted prompts respect the comfort settings of everyone involved
🔁 Consecutive Truth limit — choose how many Truths a player may select before a Dare is required
⚡ Reverse Dare — after a chosen number of consecutive dares, let a player assign a dare to another randomly selected player

💎 Premium is available through monthly and yearly subscriptions or a lifetime one-time purchase. Current prices and terms are displayed by Google Play before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Google Play. Purchases can be restored from the app.

📴 The game works locally and can be played offline. Following a successful Google Play verification, Premium access can remain available offline temporarily.

🛡️ PRIVACY

Player names, preferences, comfort settings, custom cards, and game progress are stored locally on your device. Truth or Dare has no ads, no analytics, and no developer-operated account or gameplay server.

🤝 PLAY RESPONSIBLY

Truth or Dare is intended for adults. Every activity should be voluntary and consensual. Anyone can skip a prompt, and play should stop whenever someone is uncomfortable.
